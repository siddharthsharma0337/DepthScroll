You are an award-winning Creative Frontend Engineer, Three.js developer, GSAP animation expert, cinematic motion designer, WebGL engineer, and UX designer.

Your task is NOT to create a normal website loader.

Your task is to create the opening cinematic of an immersive storytelling experience called **DepthScroll**.

The loader should feel like the first scene of a AAA game or a Netflix documentary, not a loading spinner.

=========================================================
CORE CONCEPT
=========================================================

The user is not waiting for a page.

The user is unlocking an ancient submerged gateway before beginning a descent into the ocean.

The loader IS the beginning of the story.

The transition from loader into the website must feel like one continuous camera movement.

There should NEVER be a visible page transition.

No fade to white.
No fade to black.

Only one uninterrupted cinematic shot.

=========================================================

=========================================================

The screen starts almost completely black.

NOT fantasy.

NOT medieval.

Think:

Bronze engineering

Lost civilization

Everything must feel physically believable.

=========================================================
LOADING PROGRESS
=========================================================

The loading progress controls the ancient mechanism.

Do NOT use a loading bar.

Do NOT use a spinner.

Do NOT use percentages.

The mechanism itself communicates progress.

---------------------------------------------------------

0%

Entire mechanism is locked.

Only ambient movement.

Particles.

Fish.

---------------------------------------------------------

20%

Outer bronze ring begins rotating.

Very slowly.

Heavy resistance.

Deep grinding sound.

Sediment falls.

Tiny bubbles escape.

---------------------------------------------------------

40%

Second ring rotates.

Ancient engraved symbols align.

More debris falls.

Tiny fish scatter.

---------------------------------------------------------

60%

Mechanical locking pins retract.

Heavy metallic sounds.

Pressure vibrations.

Subtle camera shake.

---------------------------------------------------------

80%

Center mechanism slowly pushes inward.

Entire hatch vibrates.

Sand erupts from edges.

Ancient pressure seal begins breaking.

---------------------------------------------------------

100%

Everything becomes still.

One second of silence.

Then...

The enormous hatch slowly opens.

The opening reveals beautiful blue ocean light.

The camera continues moving forward through the opening.

This movement seamlessly transitions into the homepage hero section.
